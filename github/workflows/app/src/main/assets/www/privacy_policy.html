Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy explains what KAVACH 4.0 Voice Assistant ("the app") does and does not do with your data.

What the app is

The app is an offline reference and training-recall tool covering KAVACH 4.0 subsystems, startup procedure, operating modes, SOS handling, collision scenarios, and safety rules for loco pilots. It is a training aid only and does not replace official railway instructions, manuals, G&SR, or OEM instructions.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you use the voice question feature, the app requests microphone access and uses your device's built-in Android speech recognition service to convert your spoken question to text on-device or via your device's configured speech-recognition provider. Audio is not recorded, stored, or transmitted by this app. You can deny microphone permission and use the text input field instead — all features remain available.

Text-to-speech

Spoken answers are read aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. This happens locally on your device.

Knowledge base content

All KAVACH reference content is bundled with the app and works fully offline. No account, sign-in, or internet connection is required to browse, search, or read topics.

Advertising

The app shows a banner advertisement at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers and other data as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data collection disclosures to serve and measure ads. We do not control what AdMob collects; please review Google's policies for details. We do not use ad clicks to influence app functionality, and the app remains fully usable if an ad fails to load.

Data we collect directly

The app stores your selected language (English/Hindi) locally on your device only, so it's remembered next time you open the app. We do not collect names, emails, contacts, location, or any other personal information.

Children

The app is a technical reference tool intended for railway loco pilots and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be bundled with the next app update and published at the URL listed in the app's Play Store listing.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the support email listed on the app's Google Play Store page.
